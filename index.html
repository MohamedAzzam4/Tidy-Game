Tide & Tidy

The tide went out and left a mess. Sort every critter into its jar, every jar into its ledger niche — and let the sound tell you you're doing it right.

Every tide is generated from a code. Share the code, share the exact same shore.

How the shore works

  • Critters → jars. Every critter wears its species and jar number. Drop it on that exact jar — the plop climbs a pentatonic scale as the jar fills.
  • Sealed jars → cubbies. A full jar pops its lid. Carry it to the ledger niche with the same species picture.
  • Double-click / double-tap any loose critter or sealed jar — it flies home on its own. Dragging is optional.
  • Pan & zoom: drag empty sand, WASD / arrows, mouse wheel, pinch. 0 re-fits the view.
  • Skills (1–3) unlock as your XP grows — carried across tides: Both Paws, Moonlight, Tide Pull, Gull's Eye, Slipstream.
  • Golden pearls are rare (1 in 25) and worth 10 XP. Streaks of 5 perfect filings chime.
  • Sound: music and ocean toggle separately (M / N) — leave the ocean on, it is the good part.
