How to play

  1. File motes into bells. A mote belongs in the bell with the same glyph and same number of pips. Drag it over the bell, or tap it, then tap the bell.
  2. Seat full bells into niches. The stone wall holds one niche per bell — same glyph, same pips. Carry the bell down.
  3. Build a streak. Every correct filing rings one note higher on a pentatonic ladder. A wrong drop only softens it — nothing is ever taken from you.
  4. Watch for Lumen motes. The prismatic ones hum. They pay triple pearls.
  5. Spend pearls on Attunements — Lantern Hand, Double Sip, Echo Pulse and friends — between tides.

Press E for your active Attunement, M to mute, Esc to put a mote down. Inspired by “Filing Good: Tidy Up” by Inglobrio — same cozy two-tier filing loop, a different ocean.
