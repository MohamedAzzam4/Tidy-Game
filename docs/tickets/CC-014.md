## Behavior this ticket owns

HUD and end-of-level presentation under the new rules:

- **Collection chips**: three colored dots (red/green/blue) next to the progress
  counter; filled = currently complete; quiet un-fill on a committed break. Rendered
  from `collectionStatus()` on committed changes only — never a correctness hint
  during drag (§8.4 Q2: pickup does not recompute; exact put-backs and
  cancel-restores produce no transition).
- **Progress semantics**: `placed` = items on **non-floor** valid placements (slot or
  surface; floor rests keep `kind: 'floor'` and do not count — C-21), `total` =
  `level.items.length` (28). The HUD and the completion card must agree (named
  HUD-vs-card consistency test).
- **Completion card**: "Home, at last." appears only on the model's completed
  transition under the tidy-all rule; "Play again" = New Mess (per CC-013).
- **Hint copy + seed display**: updated intro hint; current seed shown subtly
  (title attribute) for reproducibility.

## Likely files

- `src/style.css`

## Interfaces / data contracts

Chips consume `collectionStatus()` snapshots; progress consumes `progress()`;
completion consumes `DropResult.completed`. No new queries invented in the view.

## Dependencies

CC-005 (HUD lives beside the new ghost/drop work), CC-007 (semantics), CC-013
(Play-again = New Mess).

## Acceptance criteria

- Chip fills/stays filled exactly when its collection is currently complete; breaking
  quietly un-fills it; a held member does not change the chip until the drop commits.
- Card never appears while any item rests on the floor, even if all collections are
  complete; HUD count and card gate agree in every tested state.
- No new correctness hints introduced anywhere in the HUD.
