## Behavior this ticket owns

Authors the physical data for free placement: `SurfaceDef` entries for every approved
flat top **except** the bookshelf tiers (owned by CC-010), the furniture footprint
AABB table used for floor exclusion, and the item footprint values per category and
orientation. All numbers must match the drawn art (cross-checked against the asset
builders in `src/view/assets.ts`).

## Dependencies

## Acceptance criteria

- Every approved surface has a registry entry whose `topZ` equals the drawn top face
  (±0.02) and whose `clearance` accounts for anything drawn above it.
- Every item category + orientation has a footprint entry; the upright book entry is
  0.20 wide (builder-verified) — the plan's Q3 rule: if a value and the art disagree,
  the art wins and the data changes, never the reverse.
- Static-prop subtraction: a drop point that only overlaps a static prop's AABB is
  rejected (named test per prop).
- Sanity unit tests encoded: a mug (⌀0.21) fits the stool AABB; a towel (0.36 × 0.28)
  does not — the "sofa cannot fit in a cabinet" class of rule, concretely; a flat
  book fits the linen upper board; an upright book does not (clearance 0.23).
- No surface bounds contradict their furniture's excluded floor footprint; the entry
  mat produces no surface entry and no floor exclusion.
