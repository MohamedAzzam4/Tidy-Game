## Behavior this ticket owns

Proves the contracted playable slice end-to-end — the first stop on the way to the
full upgrade. One book can be:

1. **left on the floor** at a new position (physically valid, organizationally
   irrelevant) — it stays exactly where dropped;
2. **left on the counter** — organizationally wrong but physically valid, no
   correctness feedback of any kind;
3. **placed on a bookshelf slot** — upright, home placement, progress increments;
4. restored by an **invalid drop** (occupied slot / footprint overlap / undersized
   surface) and by a **cancelled drag** (Escape / pointercancel) to its exact previous
   location;
5. **re-dragged** after being placed (from shelf and from counter — surface re-drags
   aim through the `__cozy.itemClientPos` surface branch shipped by CC-005, C-13).

## Likely files
