## Behavior this ticket owns

Collection semantics in the pure model, implementing the user's accepted decisions
(including revision-round-1 decision Q1 = C-1(a), 2026-11-15):

- **Tier-clean rule (D2 as revised)**: a collection is *complete* iff its four
  same-color books are grouped together on **one tier** of the bookshelf — any of
  the four tiers; tiers are interchangeable; each collection may go on any eligible
  tier — **and nothing unrelated shares that tier** (the tier holds exactly those
  four books: no other books, no non-book items). **Tiers are evaluated
  independently**: what rests on other tiers (mixed colors, incomplete groups,
  foreign items) has no effect on this collection's status. Up to three collections
  can be complete at the same time.
- **Physical acceptance everywhere**: books of any color may share a tier; a tier
  with three green + one red accepts all four physically and gives **no rejection,
  warning, correctness hint, or celebration**. A tier with all four green plus an
  unrelated extra item on **that same tier** does not count for green; other tiers
  are unaffected.
- **Quiet breaking**: removing any member from the tier (or adding an unrelated item
  to it) silently flips the collection to incomplete — no event, no sound. A
  committed move that breaks a collection quietly stops counting it.
- **Completion evaluation (decision Q2)**: status is recomputed only on committed
  changes (a committed drop or a committed restore), never on pickup, never
  mid-drag; while an item is held, statuses hold their last committed values. An
  **exact put-back** (same location kind + coordinates as before the pickup) and a
  **cancel-restore** produce **no** incomplete→complete transition (the status never
  dropped), so they never re-fire the celebration; a genuine re-group after a real
  break reports the transition again, once.
- **Tidy-all win rule (accepted)**: the whole level completes only when every item
  sits on a valid non-floor placement (slot or surface — not floor, not held) **and**
  all three collections are complete **at the same time**. Note: the entry mat is
  walkable floor (no surface entry, C-22), so a mat rest counts as a floor rest and
  does **not** satisfy tidy-all; the shoes' home slots (on the mat zone) do.
- Evaluation happens on **committed changes** only, never during drag.

The cleanliness predicate, precisely (per tier, evaluated independently): the
collection's four books all rest on one tier `T` and every item on `T` belongs to
that collection. No per-shelf condition exists — other tiers are never read.

## Acceptance criteria

- 3 green + 1 red on one tier: accepted, no completion reported, no feedback.
- 4 green grouped on a tier + an unrelated item **on that same tier**: green NOT
  complete.
- 4 green grouped on a tier + an unrelated item **on another tier**: green IS
  complete (tiers evaluated independently).
- Same grouping on **any** of the four tiers: complete (tiers interchangeable).
- **Two collections simultaneously**: 4 red on tier 1 + 4 green on tier 2 ⇒ both
  complete.
- **Three simultaneously** (the winning arrangement, plan §8.4): 4 red on tier 1 +
  4 green on tier 2 + 4 blue on tier 3 + all 16 non-books in home slots ⇒ level
  complete under tidy-all.
- Removing one book from a complete collection: status silently false, no event.
- Re-grouping afterwards: completes again (reports the transition once).
- Pick up a book from a complete tier and put it straight back: status stays
  complete across the committed put-back; `collectionCompleted` stays empty (no
  re-celebration).
- Cancel-restore of a member: same — no transition, no re-celebration.
- A book left on the counter blocks level completion under tidy-all; a mat rest
  also does not satisfy tidy-all (the mat is walkable floor).
- All new logic pure (node-env testable).
