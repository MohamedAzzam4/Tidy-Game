## Behavior this ticket owns

The additional self-authored SVG artwork, keeping the existing warm, tactile art
direction (walnut / cream / teal / terracotta palette, `shade()`-derived tones):

- **Book collections**: cover color fixed per collection — red / green / blue derived
  from palette-compatible accents (terracotta is warm-red; pick a leaf-family green;
  a deep teal/blue that stays cozy) — with per-book height/thickness variation kept.
- **Spine markings in addition to color** (explicit user requirement): readable
  markings at game scale, e.g., differing cream band count (1/2/3) plus a small title
  dash on the +x spine; upright and flat orientations both marked.
- **Variant expansion**: enough distinct variants for 4 mugs, 4 cushions, 4 shoes,
  4 towels (palette reshuffles + pattern tweaks; no new drawing paradigm).

## Likely files

- `src/view/assets.ts`
- `src/view/palette.ts` (new accent constants only if needed)

## Interfaces / data contracts

- `BOOK_VARIANTS` becomes collection-aware: `variant = collectionIndex * 4 + i`,

- 12 book variants render correctly flat and upright; markings visible at the default
  camera scale and distinguishable between collections at 100% zoom.
- 16 non-book variants render without asset-key collisions.
- No third-party assets; no clashing hues (palette + `shade()` only).
