Delivery No. 1 Stock the shoals

Tide & Tidy

A calm aquarium stocking sim. A delivery of sleepy fish sits bagged on the counter. Read each tag, release every fish into the water layer it prefers, and watch the shoals form. No timers. No losing. Just water.

Best with sound on — the splashes are the game.

How to play

Every bag's tag shows which water layer its fish prefers — sunlit top, open water, or the sandy floor. A little window on the bag shows who is inside.
Drag a bag into the water and let go in that layer. The fish plops in, settles, and joins its shoal.
Wrong layer? Nothing is lost. The fish just hovers, puzzled — scoop it back out and try again. Drops on the counter re-bag safely.
As you stock the tank you learn keeper's skills: a wide net that scoops a whole species at once, a tag reader, and soft currents that settle fish quickly.
